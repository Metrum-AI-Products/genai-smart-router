# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the GenAI Smart Router community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include showing
empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and
focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
trolling or personal attacks, publishing another person's private information,
and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue. Until the project
publishes a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting address, use GitHub's
private platform reporting controls for abusive content. The repository owner
must publish the project-specific confidential enforcement contact before this
policy is considered fully operational.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers should consider the context, severity, frequency, and impact of a
violation. Possible responses include a private correction, a warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Reports and
reporter identities should be shared only with the people needed to review and
resolve the matter, subject to applicable law and platform requirements.
