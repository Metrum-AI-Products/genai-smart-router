# Subprocessor List

Current as of 2026-08-19. The applicable executed agreement and deployment
scope control.

- **Hosting and infrastructure providers:** Metrum may use customer-approved
  cloud, network, storage, monitoring, and support infrastructure to operate a
  managed instance. The specific providers and regions are disclosed during
  procurement because they depend on the selected deployment.
- **ElevenLabs:** processor for the optional ConvAI support widget on hosted
  documentation. The widget loads only after affirmative consent and is not
  part of router request processing.
- **Model providers:** providers selected and credentialed by a customer,
  including BYOK providers, are customer choices rather than Metrum
  subprocessors unless Metrum operates or contracts that provider as part of
  the managed service.

Request the engagement-specific subprocessor schedule from
[contact@metrum.ai](mailto:contact@metrum.ai).
